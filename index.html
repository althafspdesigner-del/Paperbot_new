Every layer of the brokerage, in one stack.

A single source of truth for every lead, client and account. Pipelines, tasks, automations and client timelines for sales and retention teams.

  • Retention journeys & tasks
  • Email, SMS and call logging

IB & Partner Program

Trading platform connectivity

Payments & wallets

Open API

REST APIs and webhooks so your own apps, bots and partners plug straight into the platform.

03 / How it works

From first lead to funded trade.

  1. 01

    Onboard

    Capture leads from your site, ads and partners. Automated KYC, e-signatures and welcome journeys turn applications into verified clients.

  2. 02

    Fund

    Clients deposit through your connected PSPs into multi-currency wallets, with approval rules and alerts your finance team controls.

  3. 03

    Trade

    Trading accounts are created on your platforms. Balances, trades and margin stream back into the CRM in real time.

  4. 04

    Scale

    IB structures, bonus engines, retention automations and BI dashboards grow with every new region, brand and desk.

06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Book a demo and ask us anything.

Is PAPERBOT a white-label solution?

Yes. The Trader's Room, client portal, emails and documents carry your brand, your domain and your languages. Your clients only ever see your brokerage.

Which trading platforms do you connect to?

PAPERBOT is built to connect with the leading Forex and CFD trading platforms. Tell us your stack during the demo and we'll confirm the exact bridges and sync options you need.

Can we migrate from our current CRM?

Yes. We map and import your clients, accounts, partners and history, and can run both systems in parallel until you're ready to cut over.

How long does onboarding take?

It depends on the integrations, branding and data-migration scope you choose. We'll map out a go-live plan for your brokerage during the demo.

Is the platform secure and compliant?

Role-based access, two-factor authentication, encrypted data and full audit trails are standard. KYC/AML workflows are configurable to your regulators' requirements.

Do you support multiple brands or regions?

Yes. Run several brands, entities and regions from one back office, each with its own branding, languages, currencies and partner structures.
